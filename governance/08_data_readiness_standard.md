# Data Readiness Scorecard Standard

Critical Data Element (CDE) phải có: definition, owner, steward, system of record, lineage/source, classification, retention, access rule, quality dimensions và SLA.

## DQ dimensions
Completeness, validity, uniqueness, accuracy, consistency, timeliness, reconciliation.

## Traffic light
- GREEN: mọi critical threshold đạt.
- AMBER: chưa đạt nhưng experiment vẫn an toàn, có remediation/guardrail rõ.
- RED: dữ liệu có thể làm sai outcome/control material => không qua G1/G3.
