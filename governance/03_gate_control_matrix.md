# GOV-GCM-001 — Gate Control Matrix (v0.3 Final Candidate)

## Rule
Gate decision phải dựa trên mandatory evidence và threshold đã khai báo trước. `As applicable` chỉ được dùng khi **risk tier/domain rule** chỉ ra control không áp dụng và lý do được ghi trong Artifact Register.

| Gate | Mandatory evidence | Objective threshold / rule | Sign-off & veto | Non-waivable | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G0 Portfolio Entry | Charter; strategic priority; sponsor/process owner; RISK-SCORE-001; rough business case; capacity/dependency; discovery funding request | Mandatory fields complete; tier assigned; owner accepts accountability; capacity exists for discovery | Sponsor + Process Owner + Portfolio/CEO delegate; R3/R4 Risk challenge | No anonymous initiative; no unclassified risk | Discover / Hold / Reject |
| G1 Experiment Ready | Baseline + source refs; Data Readiness Score; hypothesis; metric stack; sample/window; confounds; fallback/stop rule; experiment funding tranche | Primary metric + guardrails have formula/source/threshold; sample/window declared before run; critical CDE not RED; rollback possible | Process Owner + Transformation; R2 IT/Data as needed; R3/R4 Line-2 Risk/Finance/IT sign-off | No experiment that can cause uncontrolled material harm; no missing primary baseline | Run / Revise / Stop |
| G2 Evidence | Evidence Pack ID; baseline/result; source/artifact refs; sample/window; confounds; guardrails; cost; Condition Register | PASS only if declared primary target met and no mandatory guardrail breached. CONDITIONAL only for non-critical conditions with owner/due date; FAIL if quality/risk guardrail breached; INCONCLUSIVE if evidence validity insufficient | Sponsor + Process Owner + Transformation; Finance validates material economic claim; R3/R4 Line-2 challenge | Guardrail breach cannot be re-labeled PASS; raw/source lineage cannot be waived | Pass / Fail / Inconclusive / Conditional |
| G3 Production Ready | Approved TO-BE; control map; DE tests; all blocking G2 conditions closed/accepted; architecture/security/privacy; **Environment Representativeness Statement**; RES-ANNEX with **RTO/RPO Measurement Context**; service owner; support; cutover/rollback drill; training/adoption readiness; production funding tranche | All tier-mandatory controls DE=PASS; no expired HIGH condition; Environment Class declared; SANDBOX cannot satisfy production-resilience claim; R2-R4 recovery/rollback evidence has unit/start-stop/dependency scope; adoption readiness threshold met; production funding approved | Process Owner + Service Owner + IT; R3 Finance/Risk/Privacy as domain; R4 executive + independent QA | R4 non-waivable controls; unresolved critical control failure; no service owner; no rollback/fallback; no environment/equivalence statement for R2-R4 | Deploy / Remediate / Stop |
| G4 Benefits & Scale | 30/60/90 Benefit Ledger with **evidence grade**; Finance validation; **OE Sampling Plan created before selection**; OE results; adoption/bypass; incidents; recurring cost; residual risk; funding/scale request | Critical controls OE=PASS for predeclared population/period/method/sample; residual risk within appetite; benefit grade not overstated; only BOOKED can be realized hard cash; no material uncontrolled incident; scale funding approved | Sponsor + Process Owner + Finance; Service/Risk/IT challenge; R4 independent periodic QA | Post-hoc sample selection cannot support OE assurance; MODELED/OBSERVED cannot be labelled realized hard cash; cannot scale with failed critical OE control | Scale / Optimize / Hold / Retire |

## Conditional decision rule
- Mọi condition có Condition ID, owner, due date, impact, closure evidence và blocking gate.
- Condition HIGH/critical không được mở khi qua G3.
- Condition quá hạn tự động chuyển gate status thành HOLD cho tới khi được đóng hoặc có formal Risk Acceptance hợp lệ.
